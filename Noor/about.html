About This Project عن هذا المشروع

The story behind نور — who made it, why, and what's next.

تعلم لغة يعني أن تحمل جزءًا من ثقافة جديدة

"Learning a language means carrying a piece of a new culture."

نور (Noor, meaning "light") started as a school project with a simple idea: Arabic shouldn't feel intimidating to learn. It should feel like something you get to explore — one letter, one word, one small win at a time. What began as an assignment slowly grew into a full learning platform, built with a lot of care by three students who wanted to make something genuinely useful, not just something to hand in.

Why We Built This App لماذا صنعنا هذا التطبيق

Arabic is a language full of history, meaning, and identity — but a lot of learning tools out there make it feel like a wall of grammar rules to memorize. We wanted to flip that. We built نور because we believe learning Arabic should feel encouraging, not overwhelming: something you look forward to opening, not something you dread.

This project reflects what the three of us care about most — education, technology, and the Arabic language itself. We wanted to bring those together into something that felt useful, engaging, and culturally meaningful, not just another static website.

Who It's For لمن هذا الموقع

نور is for any student who wants to learn Arabic — whether that's for school, for connecting with family and heritage, for religious study, or simply out of curiosity about the language and its culture. We designed it for complete beginners first: no prior knowledge needed, just a willingness to start with the alphabet and build from there.

Letters, reading, writing, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, and culture are all woven together, so learning never feels like just one thing — it feels like getting to know a whole language and the people who speak it.

How We Built It كيف بنيناه

نور was built using Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant, as our main development partner — helping us design, write, and refine every page, feature, and interaction — alongside Bolt.new, a rapid web-app development tool that helped us prototype and bring ideas to life quickly. Together, these tools let three students without a professional engineering team design and build a genuinely full-featured learning platform: interactive letters and quizzes, a pronunciation checker, letter and word tracing, games, printable worksheets, and more.

Every word of Arabic content, every lesson, and every design choice was reviewed and shaped by us — the tools helped us build faster, but the vision stayed our own.

What's Next ما القادم

This is just the beginning. نور is a living project, and we have a long list of ideas we'd love to bring to life: more lessons across every grade level, new games, additional printable worksheets, richer cultural content, and tools that make it easier for teachers to support their students directly through the platform. Every version of نور will keep building on the same goal — making Arabic learning a little more joyful for the next student who visits.
