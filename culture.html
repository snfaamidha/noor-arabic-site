Culture الثقافة

The traditions and identity behind the language.

National Day اليوم الوطني

Every December 2nd, the UAE celebrates the anniversary of its founding. Streets fill with flags, fireworks, and traditional dress as families come together to mark the occasion.

Hospitality الضيافة

Welcoming guests with Arabic coffee (qahwa) and dates is a cherished tradition, symbolizing generosity and respect for visitors.

Arabic Calligraphy الخط العربي

Arabic script has long been treated as an art form, with different calligraphic styles used to decorate mosques, books, and public spaces.

The Quran and Arabic القرآن واللغة

Classical Arabic is the language of the Quran, and learning to read it connects students directly to over a thousand years of religious and literary tradition.

Traditional Dress الزي التقليدي

The kandura for men and the abaya for women remain everyday attire in the UAE, reflecting both practicality in the desert climate and cultural pride.

Desert Heritage تراث الصحراء

Falconry, camel racing, and desert camping remain popular ways Emirati families stay connected to their Bedouin roots.
