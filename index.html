Why Arabic?

A few reasons this language matters, beyond the classroom.

  • It is our language and part of our identity.

    Arabic carries the history, poetry, and voice of a whole region and its people.

  • It helps us read the Quran and understand our culture.

    Reading Arabic opens direct access to religious texts, classical literature, and tradition.

  • It is important for study and life in the UAE.

    Arabic is the national language, used in schools, government, and everyday life across the Emirates.
